NASA C-MAPSS · Time-Series Regression

Demand Forecasting

Hourly and daily demand forecasting for Capital Bikeshare (Washington D.C.). SARIMA + XGBoost ensemble captures commuter peaks, weather effects, and seasonal trends.

17,379 Hourly Records
~30 MAE (rides/hr)
