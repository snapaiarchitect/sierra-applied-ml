📊 Key Findings

Peak demand occurs at 8 AM and 5-6 PM (commute hours), with 3× higher volume than off-peak. Fall accounts for 32.2% of annual rentals — the highest season. Temperature shows a positive correlation (r ≈ 0.63) with rentals up to 27°C, then drops as heat becomes prohibitive. Calendar patterns (day of week, month) explain 73% of demand variance, making them the dominant forecasting signal.
