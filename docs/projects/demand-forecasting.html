Ensemble Forecast — Actual vs. Predicted

This is the core deliverable: a 30-day holdout forecast (December 2012) comparing four models against actual hourly bike rentals. The ensemble (stacked RF + XGB + SARIMA) reduces error by combining strengths: RF handles non-linearities, XGB captures seasonality, SARIMA models trend.
Insight: XGBoost tracks actual demand within ±25 rides/hour on 85% of test points. The ensemble smooths out RF over-predictions and SARIMA under-predictions on holiday days. Click the legend to toggle models.

📊 Model Comparison — MAE / RMSE / MAPE

Insight: XGBoost wins on all three metrics. Seasonal Naive = baseline; anything below it adds real value. Random Forest is close — good for interpretability when XGBoost is too complex.

📉 Residual Distribution — XGBoost Error

Insight: Residuals are centered near zero with ±50 rides 95% of the time. Right tail = holiday spikes (Christmas Eve, New Year's Eve) where demand is anomalously low. These are known outliers — exclude or flag.

🌸 Seasonal Demand Pattern

Insight: Summer average 234 rides/hour vs Winter 111 rides/hour — a 2.1× swing. Fall is nearly as strong as summer (218). Spring is the recovery season (164).

🕐 Hourly Demand by Weekday

Insight: Weekdays show sharp rush-hour peaks (8 AM, 5–6 PM). Weekends are flatter, peaking at 1–3 PM (leisure). Operational implication: Different rebalancing strategies for weekdays vs weekends.

Feature Importance — What Drives Demand?

These are the top features learned by XGBoost. Lag features dominate because yesterday's demand at the same hour is the strongest predictor. Cyclical encodings (hour_sin, hour_cos) let the model learn smooth daily patterns without one-hot bloat.
Insight: Hour of day (cyclical) is the #1 feature — demand is fundamentally time-of-day driven. Temperature is #2 — confirms weather impact. Lag-168 (1 week ago) is #3 — the strongest autoregressive signal.
