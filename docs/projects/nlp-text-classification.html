Category Distribution

The 20 Newsgroups dataset is the classic NLP benchmark. Categories span computers, recreation, science, politics, religion — a natural hierarchy that models capture implicitly through TF-IDF similarities.
Insight: comp.graphics and rec.sport.hockey are the largest categories (6.2% each). talk.politics.guns is the smallest (3.1%). The 60/40 train/test split preserves these proportions.

🔤 Top TF-IDF Features by Category

Insight: Domain words dominate — "space" (sci.space), "graphics" (comp.graphics), "game" (rec.sport.hockey). These are your production features. No noise, no hallucinations.

⚡ Model Comparison — Accuracy by Category

Insight: Naive Bayes matches SVM on large categories but falls 15% on rare classes (talk.politics.*). F1-score is the honest metric for imbalanced data.

🎯 Confusion Matrix (5 Super-Categories)

Insight: Misclassifications cluster within super-categories: comp.* ↔ comp.*, sci.* ↔ sci.*. A hierarchical model would fix this — classify super-category first, then sub-category.

⚡ Model Comparison Radar

Insight: Naive Bayes wins on Speed (400×) and Interpretability. SVM wins on Accuracy. Trade-off: 21% accuracy for 400× speed. Choose based on deployment constraints.
